About HABBA KHATOON SAFFRON HUB

Our Beginnings

A family business rooted in Kashmir's saffron tradition, growing into a trusted name for natural, premium products.

Our Mission

To deliver the finest Kashmiri saffron along with premium dry fruits and nuts sourced from trusted growers. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, supporting sustainable farming practices, and promoting healthy living through natural and authentic products.

Our Vision

To become a trusted name for premium saffron, dry fruits, and natural foods, recognized for quality, sustainability, and customer trust. We aim to make healthy, natural products accessible while encouraging responsible farming and preserving the richness of nature for future generations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our pure Kashmiri products? Find answers here.

1. How can I verify that your Saffron is 100% pure?

Our saffron is directly harvested from our family fields in Chandhara, Pampore. Pure saffron threads release a rich golden-yellow color slowly in warm water (not instantly red) and retain their natural crimson hue and bitter-sweet aroma.

2. How should I store my dry fruits and saffron?

To maintain freshness and natural quality, store them in a cool, dry place inside clean, airtight glass containers. Refrigeration is highly recommended, especially since our products are 100% natural and free from chemical preservatives.

3. What are your shipping charges?

We charge a flat rate of ₹100 per kilogram calculated automatically based on your order weight. We also offer FREE shipping on all orders over ₹6000!

4. How can I track my order?

You can easily track your dispatch using our India Post tracking system in the footer or menu by entering your official consignment number.

5. What is your return and refund policy?

We accept returns within 5 days of delivery for unopened and unused items in original packaging. For food items, opened packages cannot be returned due to health and safety regulations.
